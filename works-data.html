Research · Machine learning

Rice disease detection, from field to phone

An IoT system that reads soil moisture, NPK, temperature and humidity, then pairs that with image-based disease detection and a rule-based recommendation engine, so a farmer gets advice and not just a reading.

I fine-tuned a MobileNetV2 model on 22,688 images across 15 rice diseases. It reached 96.42% accuracy and runs real-time inference.

  • MobileNetV2
  • Transfer learning
  • IoT sensors
